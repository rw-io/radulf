When a delivery worker dies (SIGKILL/OOM) after `completeApproval` has already moved the card to `done` and written its approved `reviews` row, the stale reaper in `reapStaleRuns` now finishes that `review_deliveries` row as landed (`finished`, `ok = 1`, no error) and emits `review.decided` with `recoveredAfterWorkerLoss: true` instead of recording a failed merge and a stray "press Retry merge" message on a finished card; every other dead-worker delivery keeps today's failure path and the lease is still released.

The retention sweep `removeAbandonedWorktrees` is renamed `removeFinishedWorktrees` and widened from `abandoned` cards to all `FINISHED_STATUSES` (`done` + `abandoned`); for each unreclaimed `worktrees` row it deletes the run's integrity baseline first, then removes the worktree directory, its `ralph/*` branch and stamps the row — so the artefacts a dead delivery worker left behind are reclaimed on the next worker pump tick. Tests cover the landed-recovery reaper case, done-card reclaim vs. an untouched `review` card, idempotency, and the baseline-before-worktree ordering.
