Graceful worker shutdown (`SIGTERM`/`SIGINT`) now waits for a review delivery this worker has claimed — the merge, push or pull-request step of an approved card — instead of exiting mid-`mergeBranch` and leaving the card to the stale-worker reaper. `Orchestrator.hasInFlightWork()` gains a private `ownsRunningDelivery()` check on `review_deliveries` rows with `status = 'running'` owned by this worker; `shutdown.ts` comments and log lines now name deliveries as drained work, and the deadline-elapsed line says "a run or review delivery still active". Three new tests under `describe("graceful shutdown")` cover ownership counting, an end-to-end mid-merge drain that finishes with the card `done`, and a draining worker leaving newly-queued pending deliveries unclaimed.
